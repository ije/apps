clip.mp4 (H.264 and AAC) and clip.webm (VP9 and Opus) are generated 1 second clips of
64×48 frames with RGB (224, 128, 32) at 10 frames per second and a 440 Hz tone.
tone.mp3 is the same tone alone.
switch.mp3 is 0.5 seconds of the tone in stereo at 44.1 kHz followed by 0.5 seconds in mono at 22.05 kHz.
wide.mp4 is 0.3 seconds of the same frames with a 765:4 pixel aspect ratio in the container.
